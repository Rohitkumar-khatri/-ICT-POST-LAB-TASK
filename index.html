Our Departments

Computer Science

Covers programming, algorithms, artificial intelligence, data science and computer systems, with strong focus on research and problem solving.

Software Engineering

Teaches how to design, build, test and maintain large software systems, including project management and real industry practices.

Electrical Engineering

Focuses on electronics, power systems, communication and embedded systems, supported by hands-on work in well-equipped labs.

Management Sciences

Prepares students for careers in business, finance, marketing and entrepreneurship with a technology-aware approach.
